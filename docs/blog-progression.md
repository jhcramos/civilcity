# CivilCity blog progression

## Objective

Help Sunshine Coast owners, developers and project consultants make development decisions and request a relevant civil engineering proposal. Improve qualified enquiries, not article count alone. This roadmap accompanies the Codex goal created on 28 September 2026.

This document is the current-cycle editorial roadmap. The broader commercial keyword map is `docs/seo-keyword-map.md`, the 200-article expansion queue is `docs/editorial/200-article-plan.md`, and the publishing rules are `docs/editorial/publishing-governance.md`.

## Delivery cycle: commercial foundations

- Six service pages explain project situations, engineering scope, decision outcomes, proposal inputs and fee drivers: civil advice, subdivision/ROL, stormwater, operational works, RPEQ and due diligence.
- Service pages link to relevant guides. All 46 guides lead to a proposal form with the relevant service selected.
- Contact forms accept an explicit service selection; unknown or repeated URL parameters fall back to “Not sure yet”.
- Remove internal launch instructions from public copy. Do not imply Unitywater accreditation from RPEQ registration.
- Preserve existing article and service URLs. Update service sitemap dates when substantive content changes.
- Validate generated pages, links, selected form values and deployment before marking this cycle complete.

## Quality gate for each editorial update

1. Identify the reader's decision and the appropriate service before drafting.
2. Answer the core question early; explain assumptions and when site-specific advice is needed.
3. Include a useful comparison table and an actionable project checklist where they clarify the decision.
4. Use current primary Council, Queensland, BPEQ or utility sources for regulatory claims. Record source review dates in the editorial notes.
5. Use Australian English, specific local context and realistic engineering dependencies. Do not invent approval guarantees, project experience, qualifications, fees or testimonials.
6. Add descriptive internal links to the relevant service and complementary guides; preserve existing canonical URLs.
7. Verify mobile readability, link destinations, metadata and schema. Change article modification dates only for substantive editorial revisions.

## Measurement and review cadence

At the next review after sufficient post-publication data is available, compare complete 28-day periods in Search Console using the same property, Web search type and Australia filter. Keep blog and service page segments separate. Exclude brand terms when evaluating new commercial discovery. Small samples are directional, not proof of improvement.

| Measure | Definition | Decision it informs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Priority URL coverage | Indexed status of the six service pages and six guides above, with inspection date | Investigate technical or content issues before expanding the library |
| Commercial visibility | Impressions, clicks and CTR for civil engineer, stormwater, subdivision, operational works, RPEQ and due diligence query groups | Which service/topic needs stronger relevance or a clearer result snippet |
| Landing-page performance | Clicks and impressions for each priority URL | Separate service-page opportunities from informational traffic |
| Qualified enquiries | Genuine enquiries with a project location, development need and service fit | Assess commercial quality rather than raw visits |
| Proposals and wins | Qualified enquiries progressing to proposals and accepted engagements, recorded privately | Establish actual business value of organic acquisition |

Search Console does not report enquiries, revenue or CPC. Do not present these as measured until analytics/CRM evidence exists. Use Keyword Planner for geographically relevant bid estimates; a high CPC alone does not establish profitability.

## Indexing investigation

Check sitemap processing, robots permissions, HTTP status, canonical URL and indexability before requesting a recrawl of a priority page. “Discovered, currently not indexed” is not by itself evidence of a technical block. A sitemap XML resource does not need to appear as an indexed search result. An old crawl timestamp cannot evaluate a newer editorial revision.

Do not reset validation or repeatedly submit URLs merely to make the report look complete. Record unresolved Google crawl/index decisions separately from implementation work. The next measurement review is a planned workflow, not an automatically scheduled task.
