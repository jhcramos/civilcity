## Primary sources checked

All checked 1 October 2026. Claims are deliberately limited to the general processes described; the guides do not infer site-specific approval or statutory thresholds.

- [Development.i](https://developmenti.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/): supports use of the public property/application record. It does not verify site-specific feasibility.
- [Council operational work](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/development/development-applications/operational-work): supports the distinction between a development concept and possible later infrastructure approval requirements.
- [Unitywater subdivisions](https://www.unitywater.com/building-and-developing/developing/subdivisions): supports a separate servicing pathway and proposal-specific investigation. No CivilCity accreditation is implied.
- [Council plan sealing](https://www.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/development/development-applications/plan-sealing): supports including condition compliance and completion evidence in subdivision planning.
- [Understanding the proposed planning scheme](https://haveyoursay.sunshinecoast.qld.gov.au/newplanningscheme/understanding-proposed-planning-scheme): supports treating proposed provisions separately. No adoption date or future development entitlement is asserted.
