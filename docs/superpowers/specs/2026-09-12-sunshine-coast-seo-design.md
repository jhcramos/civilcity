# CivilCity Sunshine Coast SEO Strategy Design

## Goal

Make CivilCity the most useful Sunshine Coast civil engineering website for development approvals, subdivision feasibility, stormwater, access, operational works and RPEQ-related project support.

The strategy is mixed by audience, but not equal by priority:

- 70% commercial intent: developers, planners, architects, surveyors, builders, project managers and serious investors.
- 30% homeowner and small-investor education: owners asking early feasibility questions before they know which consultant they need.

The site should not chase generic civil engineering traffic. It should own the local development-approval problem space.

## Current Site Position

CivilCity already has a strong foundation:

- 17 service pages covering civil engineering advice, preliminary reporting, MCU, ROL, detailed design, operational works, RPEQ certification, stormwater, erosion and sediment control, due diligence, access, tendering, contract administration, project management and construction supervision.
- 30 insight articles covering subdivision, due diligence, access, stormwater, overlays, plan sealing, development delays, Development.i, infrastructure charges, small lot housing and other Sunshine Coast development topics.
- Generated sitemap and robots routes.
- Service and FAQ schema on service pages.
- Article and FAQ schema on insight pages.
- Strong Sunshine Coast topical focus across copy, metadata and internal content.

The next SEO advantage will come from better information architecture, stronger commercial landing pages, locality coverage, internal linking and conversion tracking.

## Audience Model

### Primary Audience: Professional Project Teams

These users already know they need civil input or suspect a planning project has civil risk.

Examples:

- Developer checking whether a subdivision or townhouse site is viable.
- Town planner needing civil engineering support for MCU, ROL or operational works.
- Architect needing driveway, parking, stormwater or levels advice.
- Builder or project manager needing construction-phase support.
- Surveyor coordinating plan sealing, easements, frontage works or operational works.

Best pages for this audience:

- Service pages.
- Project type pages.
- Local commercial landing pages.
- Technical approval guides that point to a service.

### Secondary Audience: Homeowners and Small Investors

These users search broad questions and may not know the correct language yet.

Examples:

- "Can I subdivide my land on the Sunshine Coast?"
- "Can I build a second dwelling?"
- "Do I need council approval for another driveway?"
- "What does a flood overlay mean?"
- "Can I build a duplex on my block?"

Best pages for this audience:

- Plain-English insights.
- Feasibility checklists.
- Project type pages that translate homeowner questions into professional next steps.

## Positioning

CivilCity should be positioned as:

> Sunshine Coast civil engineering support for development approvals, subdivision feasibility, stormwater, access, operational works and RPEQ review.

This is stronger than "civil engineer Sunshine Coast" alone because it ties the service to expensive, urgent problems.

## SEO Architecture

### 1. Service Pages

Purpose: convert high-intent users.

Existing service pages should remain the commercial backbone. They should be strengthened with:

- Clear "who this is for" sections.
- "When to involve CivilCity" sections.
- More internal links to relevant insights and project types.
- Stronger final CTA by project stage.
- Local context paragraphs where useful.

Priority services:

1. Operational works applications.
2. RPEQ certification.
3. Stormwater drainage design.
4. Reconfiguration of a lot engineering.
5. Material change of use engineering.
6. Preliminary civil engineering reporting and design.
7. Engineering due diligence.
8. Sight distance assessments.
9. Car parking planning and investigations.
10. Civil engineering advice.

### 2. Project Type Pages

Purpose: bridge service language and client problems.

The current `/projects` page should evolve from a single listing into a set of indexable project type pages.

Recommended project type pages:

- `/projects/subdivision-engineering-sunshine-coast`
- `/projects/townhouse-development-engineering-sunshine-coast`
- `/projects/dual-occupancy-secondary-dwelling-engineering-sunshine-coast`
- `/projects/commercial-industrial-civil-engineering-sunshine-coast`
- `/projects/driveway-access-engineering-sunshine-coast`
- `/projects/pre-purchase-development-site-due-diligence-sunshine-coast`
- `/projects/operational-works-civil-infrastructure-sunshine-coast`

Each page should include:

- Project scenario.
- Common civil constraints.
- Approval-stage considerations.
- Typical CivilCity inputs.
- Links to relevant service pages.
- Links to relevant insight articles.
- CTA for a project discussion.

### 3. Locality Pages

Purpose: capture suburb-specific local intent without creating thin doorway pages.

Start with areas that have meaningful development, infill, access, stormwater or slope issues:

- Maroochydore.
- Buderim.
- Caloundra.
- Sippy Downs.
- Nambour.
- Palmview and Aura.
- Beerwah and hinterland growth areas.

Recommended first pages:

- `/locations/maroochydore-civil-engineer`
- `/locations/buderim-subdivision-stormwater-engineer`
- `/locations/caloundra-development-civil-engineer`
- `/locations/sippy-downs-townhouse-development-engineer`
- `/locations/nambour-subdivision-access-engineer`
- `/locations/palmview-aura-development-engineering`

Each locality page must have a real local angle:

- Typical site types.
- Slope, drainage, access, overlays, frontage or growth-area constraints.
- Relevant project types.
- Relevant services.
- Nearby examples described generically, not as fake case studies.

Avoid suburb pages that only swap the suburb name. That is SEO landfill.

### 4. Insight Clusters

Purpose: capture informational searches and route them to commercial pages.

Every insight should have a clear commercial destination. If an article cannot link naturally to a service or project type, it is probably not worth writing.

Priority clusters:

#### Subdivision Feasibility

Existing coverage is strong. Add or improve:

- Subdivision costs and civil risk on the Sunshine Coast.
- When a subdivision needs operational works.
- How slope affects subdivision feasibility.
- Easements and services in small subdivisions.
- Battle-axe access risk checklist.

#### Operational Works and Approvals

Add or improve:

- What civil drawings are usually needed for operational works.
- Common operational works information request triggers.
- Civil engineering conditions after a DA.
- Plan sealing and civil works closeout.

#### Stormwater and Drainage

Add or improve:

- On-site detention for small developments.
- Overland flow path checks.
- Flood overlay and stormwater design.
- Stormwater mistakes that delay approvals.

#### Access, Driveways and Parking

Add or improve:

- Sight distance assessment guide.
- Driveway grades and long sections.
- Second driveway feasibility.
- Swept path analysis for small developments.
- Parking layout problems in townhouse sites.

#### RPEQ and Certification

Add or improve:

- When development projects need RPEQ certification.
- What RPEQ review means in civil engineering.
- RPEQ sign-off for operational works documentation.
- Builder/developer checklist before requesting certification.

#### Property Buyer Due Diligence

Add or improve:

- Civil engineering checks before buying a development site.
- Red flags in Sunshine Coast council mapping.
- Hidden civil costs in small development sites.
- What to ask before making an offer on a subdivision site.

## Internal Linking Rules

Use a strict hub-and-spoke model:

- Each service page links to 3-6 relevant insights and 1-3 project type pages.
- Each insight links to one primary service page and one project type page.
- Each project type page links to 3-5 service pages and 4-8 insights.
- Each locality page links to the most relevant services and project types, not every page.

Preferred anchor text should be descriptive:

- Good: "operational works application support"
- Good: "stormwater drainage design for Sunshine Coast development sites"
- Bad: "click here"
- Bad: repeated exact-match anchors on every page

## Conversion Model

Every commercial page should support three actions:

1. Discuss a project.
2. Send site details.
3. Read a related guide.

Suggested form prompts:

- Site address or suburb.
- Project type.
- Current stage.
- Main issue.
- Timing.

The contact form should accept messy early-stage enquiries, because homeowners and small investors will not use consultant language.

## Authority Signals

Add authority without overclaiming:

- Clear principal/engineer profile for Juliano.
- RPEQ wording only where accurate and reviewed.
- "Sunshine Coast development approval experience" phrasing where true.
- Local project-type examples described generically.
- Links to official resources when helpful.
- Google Business Profile.
- Local citations.
- Reviews from real clients, if available.

Do not invent case studies, accreditations, approvals or project outcomes.

## Measurement

Before publishing a large batch of pages, confirm:

- GA4 is installed.
- Google Search Console is connected.
- Form submissions are tracked.
- Phone/email clicks are tracked.
- Sitemap is submitted.
- Bing Webmaster Tools is optional but low-effort.

Track:

- Queries by cluster.
- Impressions.
- Clicks.
- Average position.
- Leads by page.
- Lead quality by project type.
- Pages indexed vs submitted.

## 30/60/90 Day Strategy

### First 30 Days

Focus on technical readiness and high-intent commercial pages:

- Confirm analytics, Search Console and conversion tracking.
- Strengthen top 10 service pages.
- Improve internal links across existing services and insights.
- Create the first 3 project type pages.
- Create a keyword map.
- Submit sitemap and inspect priority URLs.

### Days 31-60

Build project-type and locality coverage:

- Create remaining project type pages.
- Create first 3 locality pages.
- Add 8-12 supporting insights based on gaps.
- Add stronger CTAs and contact-form routing.
- Review Search Console data for emerging queries.

### Days 61-90

Build authority and expand based on data:

- Create next 3 locality pages.
- Add official-resource links and authority blocks.
- Add downloadable/checklist-style lead magnets where useful.
- Refresh underperforming pages.
- Build local citations and review process.
- Decide whether to add Google Ads for the top commercial pages.

## Risks

- Too many low-quality locality pages could weaken trust.
- Homeowner traffic can create noisy enquiries if forms are not structured.
- Overusing RPEQ language could create compliance risk if wording is not reviewed.
- Creating content without tracking leads can hide whether SEO is generating revenue.
- Generic civil engineering content will not differentiate CivilCity.

## Success Criteria

CivilCity is winning if, within 6-12 months:

- Priority service pages are indexed and gaining impressions.
- CivilCity appears for long-tail Sunshine Coast approval, subdivision, stormwater and operational works queries.
- Search Console shows growth across both commercial and informational clusters.
- Leads arrive through service, project type and selected insight pages.
- The best enquiries are from developers, planners, architects, builders and serious small investors.
